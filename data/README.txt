This folder includes:

> Klytics Report 05-2025(OUTPUT).csv & Klytics Report 04-2025(OUTPUT).csv
Two consecutive monthly market reports containing real market indicators for publishing categories and niches, including estimated sales, growth, pricing, competition, Kindle Unlimited share and Sales-to-Competition Ratio. These provide the external market perspective against which Cobalt Fairy's portfolio can be examined.

> CF_Catalogue_Performance.xlsx
A prepared dataset based on 47 real Cobalt Fairy titles published during April and May 2025. It combines catalogue information such as title, author, series, publication date, genre/subgenre and trope with synthetic commercial performance data for the US Amazon marketplace.

> FairyMail_Competitor_Pricing_Sep2025.xlsx
Historical pricing snapshot prepared from FairyMail's September 2025 competitor market analysis. It is intended to serve as a baseline that can be compared with current competitor websites during the trial.

> social_posts.csv & social_coverage.csv
Captured public posts from Facebook and LinkedIn for Mailchimp, Brevo and MailerLite. LinkedIn posts were manually copied from public company pages; dates are approximate and permalinks are not captured. Instagram is unavailable. The per-brand LinkedIn captures are in linkedin_*_posts.csv and are merged into social_posts.csv.
